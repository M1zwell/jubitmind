1. **Choose a different product name** that does not contain "Jubit", "ChatAB",

2. **Replace the Jubit logo** with your own branding
3. **Update the application title** in the sidebar, page titles, and metadata
4. **Keep the NOTICE file** intact (required by Apache 2.0) — this preserves
   the attribution to Jubit AI without implying your fork IS JubitMind

```
OK:     "MyAuditTool — based on JubitMind (Apache 2.0)"
```
